### Two-phase generated evidence

The preupload attestation now contains the complete sorted `path`/`bytes`/`sha256` manifest in addition to its phase digest and count. Hosted finalization may add only the four exact hosted JSON records plus PNGs explicitly listed by the production-review manifest. Before sealing hosted-final, the scanner subtracts that closed set and both self-attestation paths, recomputes the original preupload projection and domain digest, and requires byte-for-byte manifest equality. Changed, removed, collided, extra, generic reporter-root, or unmanifested production output therefore fails. The closed production-smoke schema and persisted-output rule remove the earlier open-ended inter-phase mutation surface.
