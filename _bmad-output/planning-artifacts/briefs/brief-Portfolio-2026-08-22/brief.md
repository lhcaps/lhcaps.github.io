# Product Brief: Le Huy Systems Atlas

## Executive summary

Le Huy Systems Atlas is an English-language engineering portfolio for a junior software engineer with a full-stack, backend-leaning profile. It gives recruiters and engineers a fast path from a memorable proposition to concrete proof: a DOCX contract, a queued inference job, a pricing rule that changed four times, a capture waiting for human review, and a harness that can reject its own evidence.

The product treats the portfolio itself as a working sample of engineering judgment. One meaningful 3D atlas makes architecture tangible, while readable case studies, source-backed claims, responsive fallbacks, and release evidence carry the same story without WebGL. The primary action is **Work with me**.

## Evidence and product boundary

Repository truth, verified evidence, the latest approved specification, correctness, accessibility, and verification outrank visual preference. The evidence layer remains private. Public copy stays affirmative and literal; concrete artifacts carry personality and engineering qualities.

## The problem

Backend-heavy work hides behind interfaces. A project tile can name NestJS, Redis, or PostgreSQL while leaving the difficult part invisible: where authority changes, which state survives a retry, how a quote becomes an allocation, or why a release gate stays red.

Recruiters need a clear signal within the first viewport. Technical readers need enough depth to trust that signal. Generic developer-portfolio patterns serve neither audience: technology inventories replace decisions, repeated cards flatten different systems into one shape, and visual effects consume attention without carrying information.

## Product thesis

Concrete parts stand for the engineer:

- the contract stands for domain governance;
- the queue stands for cross-runtime architecture;
- the fourth pricing change stands for adaptation;
- the review handoff stands for operational judgment;
- the failed gate stands for verification discipline.

The site should leave three impressions: Le Huy finishes difficult systems, adapts when requirements move, and is enjoyable and trustworthy to work with. The product proves each impression through artifacts and transitions rather than adjectives.

## Who this serves

**Primary: recruiters and hiring managers.** They need Le Huy's role, orientation, strongest systems, personality, and contact path within a short scan.

**Secondary: engineering managers and interviewers.** They need architecture, constraints, failure modes, verification, and honest evidence boundaries.

**Tertiary: potential collaborators.** They need a human voice, delivery habits, public work links, and a direct Work with me action.

## The experience

1. **Opening:** Le Huy, Software Engineer, backend-leaning full-stack proposition, and Work with me.
2. **Systems Atlas:** one guided, selectable topology that reconfigures across five systems and explains the meaning of depth, links, focus, and state.
3. **Selected systems:** deep, distinct narratives for Form Management, VisionFlow Studio, and an anonymized Production Booking & Operations Platform; shorter supporting narratives for Parkly and TFT Local Copilot.
4. **Adaptation loop:** client feedback → pricing rule → implementation → verification → release → next revision, with confidential details removed.
5. **AI-assisted engineering:** intent → research → BMAD contracts → implementation → harness → review → evidence, under the thesis “The agent writes. The harness decides.”
6. **Verification and capabilities:** real gates and engineering outcomes, organized by what the system must accomplish.
7. **Closing:** Work with me, GitHub, email, and a facts-only CV assembled from verified public evidence.

## What makes it distinct

- **Architecture is the visual subject.** Depth separates authority layers, routes show real handoffs, and focus reveals dependencies.
- **Proof precedes copy.** Every material technical sentence traces to a private evidence ledger with a source, evidence tier, confidence, public-safety decision, and limitation.
- **Different systems receive different narrative forms.** Three flagship cases carry depth; two supporting cases show breadth without becoming identical cards.
- **The portfolio demonstrates its own method.** Typed content, deterministic topology checks, one verification entry point, browser QA, performance evidence, and governed deployment make the site one more verified system.
- **Voice carries personality through precision.** Energetic, direct, playful writing uses concrete engineering moments. It avoids irony, litotes, corporate vocabulary, and portfolio clichés.

## Release scope

### In scope

- English-only single-page portfolio on GitHub Pages.
- Five curated systems with three flagships.
- One lazy 3D Systems Atlas with explicit state mapping.
- Semantic 2D/text equivalents, mobile-specific composition, reduced motion, WebGL fallback, and scene error handling.
- Case-study copy backed by public-safe evidence.
- AI-assisted engineering and verification-harness narratives.
- Responsive, keyboard, focus, contrast, touch, console, link, overflow, and metadata validation.
- Correct canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, Person/ProfilePage structured data, sitemap, robots, and favicon assets.

### Out of scope

- Portraits, avatars, generated people, private client screenshots, real pricing, credentials, provider configuration, and physical-room identity.
- Localization, theme switching, CMS, blog, testimonial system, analytics product, or speculative case-study framework.
- Claims about employers, users, scale, performance, uptime, business outcomes, or production state without current evidence.
- Rive or additional 3D canvases without a proven state-machine need.

## Success criteria

- A first-time visitor can identify Le Huy, the target role, backend-leaning full-stack orientation, three flagship systems, and Work with me.
- All five systems expose a deterministic topology and a readable equivalent representation; project switching works by pointer, touch, and keyboard.
- Depth, links, selection, and motion each have documented meaning; decorative particles, bloom, orbit toys, and ambient loops are absent.
- Every material public claim maps to public-safe evidence. The client remains anonymous. Source inspection is never described as live runtime proof.
- The site passes fresh typecheck, lint, unit, build, E2E, content/topology harness, accessibility, responsive, console, and link gates.
- Browser review covers the defined viewport matrix, intermediate widths, and reduced-motion/fallback states.
- Production output reports the initial JavaScript payload, lazy 3D chunk, major assets, and build result.
- Product, visual, motion, simplicity, code, and live screenshot reviews have no unresolved material findings.
- GitHub Pages serves the final exact SHA and passes desktop/mobile production smoke without critical console errors.
