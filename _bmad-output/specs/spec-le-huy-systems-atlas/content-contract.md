## Public Verification Harness policy

The public Harness is content-graph copy, not a current release report. It lists every required gate category, describes what accepts or rejects work, and may include only the approved historical stale-summary Failed Gate with its literal `OBSERVED` or `BLOCKED` qualification. A public row contains only stable ID, category label, acceptance job, public-safe explanation, optional allowed assertion label, and required limitation. Validators reject current-completion claims and the fields `command`, `exitCode`, `count`, `sha`, `timestamp`, `workflow`, `url`, `payload`, `asset`, `review`, or `evidenceRef` from public Harness data.

Internal Release Evidence, reviewer attestations, history/confidentiality receipts, asset inventory, production screenshot evidence, and `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` remain non-deployed. The public UI does not fetch or link machine-only `release.json`; operators and the final handoff verify that endpoint separately.
