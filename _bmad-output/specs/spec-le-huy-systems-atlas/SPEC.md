# Le Huy Systems Atlas

## Why

This release realizes a vision and closes a credibility gap for recruiters, engineering interviewers, collaborators, and privacy-conscious stakeholders: Le Huy needs one public engineering portfolio that makes backend-leaning full-stack judgment inspectable, preserves confidential boundaries, works without WebGL, and earns trust through exact evidence and release gates rather than unsupported claims or visual effects.

## Capabilities

- **CAP-1 — Candidate decision surface**
  - **intent:** A reader can identify Le Huy, the Software Engineer and full-time Junior target, the backend-leaning full-stack proposition—roughly 60% full-stack product-engineering breadth and 40% backend-depth emphasis, with backend depth as the strongest technical dimension—the five-System proof index, and the exact **Work with me** action before deciding where to go next.
  - **success:** Required identity, proposition, navigation, proof index, and Primary Action remain unobscured and operable at every release viewport before the scene loads or when it fails.

- **CAP-2 — Authoritative Systems Atlas**
  - **intent:** A reader can select exactly five Systems and compare their evidence-supported layers, nodes, routes, Active Handoff, and narrative destination through one coherent Atlas model.
  - **success:** Five of five Systems pass topology validation, and the selector, Readable Equivalent Representation, narrative references, and optional scene expose the same selected state and relationships.

- **CAP-3 — Resilient focal visualization**
  - **intent:** A reader can use the focal Atlas as progressive enhancement without losing content or control under loading, narrow viewports, Reduced Motion, WebGL failure, or unavailable WebGL.
  - **success:** One lazy Canvas reserves stable space on eligible viewports, while every bypass or failure path completes the same five-System journey through semantic DOM with no layout trap or exclusive scene information.

- **CAP-4 — Evidence-bounded system narratives**
  - **intent:** A reader can inspect three distinct Flagship Narratives, two concise Supporting Narratives, and the anonymous Fourth Pricing Change loop at the evidence level each Claim actually supports.
  - **success:** Every material Claim maps to an allowed Public Claim Manifest entry with a stable key, System ID, classification, public-safe decision, and exact limitation; zero private-ledger or confidential content reaches source history, build output, or deployment.

- **CAP-5 — Controlled engineering and verification story**
  - **intent:** A reader can follow the AI-assisted lifecycle and see where authored work becomes accepted work under **THE AGENT WRITES. THE HARNESS DECIDES.**
  - **success:** The page distinguishes contracts, scoped implementation, deterministic gates, a precisely qualified Failed Gate, browser QA, review, and Release Evidence without fake terminals, tool theater, or unsupported productivity metrics.

- **CAP-6 — Outcome-led capability and contact closure**
  - **intent:** A reader can connect engineering capabilities to concrete System anchors, then reach verified email, GitHub, Work with me, and facts-only CV destinations.
  - **success:** Every destination is keyboard reachable and link-validated; the CV contains only verified public facts and omits unsupported education, employment, dates, employers, awards, metrics, and personal details.

- **CAP-7 — Publication and exact-SHA release**
  - **intent:** The repository can publish complete machine-readable identity and deploy the verified portfolio through GitHub Pages from one canonical acceptance path.
  - **success:** Metadata and static endpoints validate, all required local and hosted gates pass, Pages serves the final exact SHA, and production desktop and mobile smoke show no critical console, content, interaction, asset, or routing failure.

## Constraints

- The public experience is one English single page. The CTA is exactly **Work with me**. It uses no portrait and speaks with energetic, concrete, direct, technical, human prose led by verified artifacts and handoffs.
- One authoritative typed model supplies selector data, readable topology, scene geometry, narrative references, and Claim validation. Duplicate IDs, unresolved routes, missing evidence keys, or missing required limitations fail before build.
- There is exactly one focal Canvas. It is lazy, outside the Opening critical path, capped to DPR `[1, 1.5]`, and uses refs or renderer-local state inside frame loops. Offscreen work pauses; a second or decorative Canvas is forbidden.
- Below `768px`, under Reduced Motion, or after WebGL failure, the Canvas does not mount. Semantic DOM preserves every System, relationship, selection, evidence limitation, and destination.
- Every motion is finite, causal, interruptible, mapped to a documented state, and removable. Ambient pulse, float, shimmer, type, route, particle, bloom, and toy-orbit loops are forbidden.
- The release targets WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic ownership, keyboard and touch parity, visible focus, minimum target sizing, `4.5:1` normal text, `3:1` large text, and `3:1` meaningful non-text contrast. Canvas receives no focus and contains no exclusive information.
- Architecture binds numeric initial-JavaScript and lazy-3D budgets, measurement units, and the major-asset inventory before the first feature story. An unresolved excess fails release.
- Public evidence never upgrades source inspection to runtime or production proof. Private ledgers, client identity, prices, screenshots, credentials, provider configuration, payment details, sensitive deployment information, and physical-room identity never enter public artifacts.
- Public Claim Manifest classifications and release assertion labels are separate closed vocabularies defined in the companions.
- Canonical verification composes current typecheck, lint, unit, build, content/topology, E2E, accessibility, responsive overflow, console, link, metadata, CV, performance, hosted CI, and production attestation gates. An unrun or failing required gate forces `PARTIAL`.
- New commits use repository-local identity `Huy Le <huyle210525@gmail.com>`, exact-path staging, and no reset, clean, stash, worktree, broad secret output, or unguarded force.
- History maintenance may remove only exact authorized Cursor co-author trailer lines after an external verified bundle and identity audit. Commit order, dates, messages outside the target trailer, every tree, and genuine authorship remain unchanged; publication uses explicit `--force-with-lease` only.

## Non-goals

- Portraits, avatars, generated people, silhouettes, stock imagery, decorative 3D assets, black-neon or cyberpunk styling, glassmorphism, gradients, bento walls, fake dashboards, fake terminals, logo clouds, skill meters, metrics theater, or perpetual ambience.
- A second Canvas, decorative Rive, particle field, bloom pass, unrestricted orbit, or any 3D element without documented information meaning.
- A CMS, blog, localization, theme switcher, testimonials, analytics product, lead-capture form, technology inventory, or speculative case-study framework.
- Confidential client disclosure, exhaustive repository mirroring, or a claim that source inspection proves current runtime or production state.
- Optimization for visit duration, click count, technology-name density, effect count, or brittle full-page pixel equality.

## Success signal

The result is `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` only when a recruiter can act from the Opening, a technical reader can trace any of five Systems with or without WebGL, every Claim validates against public-safe evidence, every required gate and independent review passes, and GitHub Pages serves the final exact SHA on desktop and mobile. Any missing condition leaves the release explicitly `PARTIAL`.
